### Featured project

**[EU Cosmetics Safety Gate: a regulatory risk monitor](https://github.com/eshwarav/eu-cosmetics-safety-gate-monitor)**
A ten-KRI monitor built on 5,403 EU market-surveillance alerts in Excel, Power Query and Power BI, reconciled to the digit between the two.

- It shows that a banned ingredient keeps causing recalls for years: 72% of BMHCA alerts came two or more years after the ban.
- The emerging-substance flag caught the next wave (TPO in gel nail polish) seven months before it peaked.

`Power BI` `DAX` `Power Query` `Excel` `Star schema` `Time intelligence`
