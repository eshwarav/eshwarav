## Featured case

### EU Cosmetics Safety Gate: early warning for regulatory risk
**[View the repository →](https://github.com/eshwarav/eu-cosmetics-safety-gate-monitor)**

**Question.** Which leading indicators would warn a brand owner that a regulatory change is about to turn into recalls and write-offs?

**Approach.** I analysed every EU cosmetics safety alert from 2016 to 2026: 5,403 alerts and 6,638 enforcement measures. I built a ten-KRI monitor in Excel and Power BI, using a star schema, time intelligence and an emerging-substance flag, and reconciled every headline figure across both tools.

**Impact.**
- **Showed that a ban date starts the risk instead of ending it.** 72% of alerts for a banned fragrance ingredient came two or more years after the ban.
- **Caught the next wave early.** The emerging-substance flag fired on TPO in gel nail polish seven months before alerts peaked. A ranking by volume would have missed it.
- **Wrote it up as a client deliverable,** with an executive summary, numbered exhibits, and four recommendations each tied to a KRI.

`Power BI` `DAX` `Power Query` `Excel` `Data modelling` `KRI design`
